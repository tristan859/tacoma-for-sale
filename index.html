About this truck

Clean 2018 Tacoma TRD Off-Road Double Cab in Cement with the Black Package. Rear-wheel-drive 4x2 with the 3.5L V6 (~278 hp) and 6-speed automatic — 165,000 miles.

This is the TRD Off-Road setup buyers look for: Bilstein shocks, an electronic locking rear differential, and all-terrain tires on TRD wheels. Toyota Safety Sense P is on board (pre-collision with pedestrian detection, lane departure alert, dynamic radar cruise, and automatic high beams). Note: this is a 4x2 — it does not include Crawl Control or Multi-Terrain Select.

Inside: black leather and a sunroof. Outside: Black Package accents, fog lights, chrome side steps, and a lined short bed. Factory tow rating is about 6,400 lb. Short-bed Double Cab on a Toyota of El Cajon dealer frame.

Well cared for with no major body damage in the photos. Solid daily driver and lifestyle truck.
